REPMETRIC · iOS & Android

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

RepMetric is a workout, nutrition, and progress tracking app operated by its developer, kiskalloakos. For privacy questions or requests, contact akos@famescale.co. This policy covers the app, including prerelease versions. Feature availability may vary by version.

Information you provide

We process your account email and sign-in identifiers; your optional profile photo; profile and training preferences, including age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level and goals; workouts, sets, food logs and body measurements; and friend requests, messages and voice notes you choose to send. These details enable the features you use, such as calorie estimates, history, streaks and sharing progress.

Storage and sharing

Supabase provides account authentication, database storage and file storage. Some data is cached on your device, including entries waiting to sync. Friends can see the information you choose to share through the app's sharing controls. People with a workout-sharing link can view that shared workout. Photos, microphone access, camera access and notifications are used for the features you choose and are subject to device permissions.

Expo provides app updates and, when enabled, notification delivery. Food searches and barcode lookups may contact Open Food Facts; these requests include the search or barcode needed to return results. Shared workout pages are hosted using Vercel. Service providers may process technical information, including IP addresses and device or request details, needed to deliver and secure their services.

Advertising and your choices

The free app supports Google AdMob banner ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect ads. RepMetric does not send your workout, food, body-measurement or private message contents to AdMob for ad targeting, and does not request precise location for advertising.

Where required, Google's consent form lets you accept, reject or manage advertising purposes and partners before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Depending on your choices and region, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads. iOS tracking permission is separate and can also be managed in device Settings.

A lifetime Remove ads purchase disables banners while your entitlement is active. We do not request banners for profiles indicating an age below 18, or when age has not been provided. The app is not intended for children under 13.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes in-app payments. RevenueCat verifies purchases and maintains access to the lifetime Remove ads entitlement using an app user identifier, transaction details, product identifiers, purchase status and relevant device and store information. RepMetric does not receive your full payment-card details. Restore Purchases is available in Settings. Store and RevenueCat transaction records may be retained as needed for purchase verification, refunds, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

Use, retention and your rights

We use information to provide requested app features, maintain your account, verify purchases, respond to support requests, and secure the service. Where applicable, processing relies on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and support, legal obligations, or consent for optional processing such as advertising where required. Fitness information may be sensitive; only enter and share information you want the app to process.

You can edit or delete supported records in the app and delete your account in Settings → Delete account. You can also request account and associated-data deletion by emailing akos@famescale.co from your account email. We may need to verify your request. Account deletion removes associated app data; backups and records required for security, purchases or legal obligations may remain for the applicable retention period.

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of your personal information, object to applicable processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights. Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.
